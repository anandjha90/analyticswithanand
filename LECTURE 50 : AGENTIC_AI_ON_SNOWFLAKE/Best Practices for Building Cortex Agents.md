## Best Practices for Building Cortex Agents

## Why this matters:

The orchestration and response instructions you write directly impact agent reliability. 
Snowflake's AI team has compiled best practices based on extensive testing and real-world deployments. 
Understanding these patterns helps you avoid common pitfalls and build agents that perform consistently.

## What you'll learn:

The guide covers instruction design patterns including tool selection hierarchies, trigger word strategies, boundary setting, and response formatting. 
It also addresses common failure modes and how to prevent them through careful instruction crafting.

## How to use this resource:

This gives you context for the instruction patterns we'll implement together. As you work through the hands-on , you'll see these best practices applied to your sales intelligence agent.
